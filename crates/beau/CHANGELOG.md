## 0.1.0

First release: the foundations.

### Features

- **tokens:** `Theme` with semantic palette, type scale, spacing, radii,
  control sizes, layered elevation and motion timings, in light and dark.
  Accent colours are app-defined via `Theme::with_accent`, with derived tokens
  that meet WCAG contrast. Light/dark follows the OS by default.
- **typography:** bundled Inter variable font (Latin subset) with weights 400,
  500 and 600, display optical size for headings, and tabular figures.
- **paint:** layered soft shadows, anti-aliased gradients, physical-pixel
  hairlines and focus rings.
- **motion:** frame-rate-independent analytic springs, easing curves, and
  presence (`presence`, `PresenceList`) for animating elements in and out.
  No repaints while idle.
- **components:** `Button` (primary, secondary, ghost, danger; three sizes),
  `TextInput`, `Card`, `Dialog` (animated open and close, backdrop, Escape to
  dismiss) and `Kbd`.
